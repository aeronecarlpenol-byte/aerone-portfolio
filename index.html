Who I Am

I'm Aerone Carl P. Peñol, a first-year Electronics Engineering student at the Technological University of the Philippines–Visayas. My academic foundation is grounded in analysis, logic, and the engineering mindset of breaking complex problems into solvable parts.

I'm drawn to the intersection of hardware concepts and software tools — from engineering design software like AutoCAD and SketchUp to programming fundamentals in Python and Java. I've begun applying these skills in personal projects, including a web-based academic productivity platform where I worked on interface design and iterative problem-solving.

Outside of technical coursework, I engage actively in research, publication work, and student governance — roles that have strengthened my organizational, leadership, and communication skills. I approach learning as a continuous process and take pride in independent study and practical application.

I'm currently building toward a career in electronics engineering, with an interest in applying technical knowledge to real problems through research, design, and development.
