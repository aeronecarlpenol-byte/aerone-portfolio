# SVG brand assets

Downloaded November 9, 2026 from each brand's official website. The original
SVG geometry and colors are retained; logo files contain no external requests.

- `claude.svg`: https://claude.com/icon.svg (Claude's terracotta symbol).
- `chatgpt.svg`: https://chatgpt.com/cdn/assets/favicon.6a33781f61.svg (official
  ChatGPT blossom; its supplied dark-color-scheme variant is used on this site).
- `vercel.svg`: https://vercel.com/geist/brands/assets/vercel-symbol-white.svg
  (white symbol from Vercel's brand page).
- `supabase.svg`: https://supabase.com/brand-assets/supabase-icon-green-transparent.svg
  (green symbol from Supabase's brand page).

Marks belong to their respective owners. Their inclusion does not imply
affiliation, partnership or endorsement.

Additional Skills & Tools symbols:

- `html5.svg`, `java.svg`, `python.svg`: original-color Devicon SVGs from
  https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/master/icons (individual brand marks).
- `github.svg`: official Primer Octicons `mark-github-24.svg` from
  https://github.com/primer/octicons; white fill for the dark background.
- `codex.svg`: Codex symbol from https://devicons.io/devicons/icons/codex-icon.svg.
- `antigravity.svg`: official full-color icon from
  https://antigravity.google/assets/image/brand/antigravity-icon__full-color.svg.

Categorized workbench additions (November 9, 2026):

- css3.svg, javascript.svg, postgresql.svg: original-color SVGs from
  https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/master/icons
- autocad.svg, sketchup.svg, easyeda.svg: unmodified Simple Icons geometry
  from https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/develop/icons, with
  brand fills E51050, 005F9E, 1765F6 from its data/simple-icons.json metadata.
